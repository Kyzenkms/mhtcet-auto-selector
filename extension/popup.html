Phase 1: Prepare Your List

The parser is smart! Your list can have multiple columns (like Preference No. or College Name), but it MUST contain the Institute Code and Course Name in separate columns.

💡 Top-to-Bottom Ranking: The extension automatically ranks your colleges based on their top-to-bottom order in the file!

✅ DO (Extra Columns are Fine):
1 | 1107 | P.R Pote | Computer Engineering
❌ DON'T (No mashed-up strings):
1107 - P.R Pote Patil College (CE)

Phase 2: MHT-CET Portal Steps

Step 1 Page (Select Options)
  1. Upload your list: Go to the Auto-Select tab and upload your file.
  2. Meet your Assistant: The moment your list loads, the Live Assistant Sidebar will automatically slide in on the right side of the MHT-CET website!
  3. Select a Course: Choose a branch from the website's dropdown. The Assistant will instantly calculate and tell you exactly how many of your colleges offer that branch.
  4. Auto-Select: Click the purple ⚡ Auto-Select button inside the sidebar. It will find and check all the exact matches for you.
  5. Review: Watch the live Sidebar for 💡 Smart Advice warnings to catch any tricky branch names!
Step 2 Page (Set Preferences)
  1. Once you save and move to the Set Your Preferences page, click the green Step 2 (Order) button in the sidebar.
  2. The extension will automatically type the preference numbers (1, 2, 3...) into the correct boxes exactly following the top-to-bottom order of your uploaded file!
⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING:
The extension acts as a guardian assistant, NOT a replacement for your own judgement! ALWAYS double-check your final preference list before submitting.
